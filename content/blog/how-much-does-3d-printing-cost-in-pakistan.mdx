There is no single price, because every part is different. Custom 3D printing is usually quoted per project.

## What Affects the Price
- **Size and volume:** bigger parts use more material and print time.
- **Material:** PLA and PETG suit different jobs.
- **Complexity:** supports, fine detail and tight tolerances take longer.
- **Quantity:** multiple copies can change the per-piece cost.
- **Design work:** if you need a 3D model made, design time is included in the quote.
- **Delivery:** local pickup or courier.

## Getting an Accurate Quote
Send a description, approximate dimensions, quantity, preferred material, delivery city and deadline. A sketch or photo helps; we can ask for files on WhatsApp after your request.
