Choosing the right filament decides how your part looks, lasts and performs.

## PLA
Easy to print with sharp detail. Best for models, prototypes, display pieces and gifts. It softens in high heat, so avoid leaving it in a hot car.

## PETG
Tougher and more flexible, with better heat and moisture resistance. Best for functional parts, brackets, enclosures and outdoor use.

## Quick Rule
Looks and detail: **PLA**. Strength and durability: **PETG**. Not sure? Send us your use case and we will recommend one.
