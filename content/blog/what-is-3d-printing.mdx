3D printing, also called additive manufacturing, builds an object layer by layer from a digital 3D model.

## The Basic Process
1. **Model:** a 3D design is created or provided as an STL file.
2. **Slicing:** software cuts the model into thin layers and plans the printer's movements.
3. **Printing:** the printer melts plastic filament and deposits it layer by layer.
4. **Finishing:** supports are removed and the part is cleaned or finished.

## What It Is Good For
Prototypes, replacement parts, custom holders and enclosures, models and personalised gifts. No design yet? A 3D model can be developed from a sketch or measurements.
